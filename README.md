# FounderFlow AI 🚀
### Your AI-Powered Startup Operations Copilot

FounderFlow AI is an AI-powered productivity platform designed to help startup founders and small teams manage leads, prioritize opportunities, generate personalized follow-up emails, and organize daily tasks from one simple dashboard.

Instead of manually reviewing every inquiry and deciding what to do next, founders can use FounderFlow AI to analyze lead details, identify high-priority opportunities, prepare tailored outreach drafts, and turn important actions into organized tasks.

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI Lead Analysis:** Review lead information, understand customer intent, and prioritize potential opportunities.
- **Smart Lead Scoring:** Assign scores to leads to help identify which opportunities may deserve attention first.
- **AI Follow-Up Drafts:** Generate personalized email drafts based on lead information and outreach instructions.
- **Human Approval Workflow:** Review, edit, approve, or dismiss drafts before taking action. Emails are not sent automatically.
- **Task Management:** Organize follow-ups and important activities into actionable tasks.
- **AI Copilot:** Interact with the assistant to request lead insights, outreach drafts, and operational support.
- **Activity Tracking:** View recent lead analysis, draft preparation, and task-related activity.
- **Warm, Responsive UI:** A modern interface with a nude-inspired color palette and light/dark mode.
- **Backend API:** Manage application data and connect the frontend to AI-powered functionality.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Startup founders often manage sales inquiries, customer follow-ups, and operational tasks manually. This can lead to missed opportunities, delayed responses, and difficulty deciding which lead to prioritizeFounderFlow AI aims to simplify these workflows by bringing lead intelligence, AI-assisted communication, and task organization into a single platform.

💡 Our Solution

FounderFlow AI combines lead management with an AI-assisted workflow. It helps founders understand incoming opportunities, prepare relevant follow-up messages, and organize their next actions while keeping important decisions under human control.

The goal is not to replace the founder, but to reduce repetitive work and make daily operations more organized and efficient.
